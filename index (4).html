04 — Swiss DNA

Precision is not a value.
It is the operating condition.

Cobsistentia operates from Lugano, in the Canton Ticino — a jurisdiction chosen deliberately for its stability, discretion, and engineering culture. We work as a lean research studio: few projects, deep involvement, no intermediation between the people who design a system and the people who write it.

Intellectual property is treated as the core asset. Architectures are documented, compartmentalized, and developed under strict confidentiality standards — so that what we build remains defensible, auditable, and entirely ours or our partners'.
